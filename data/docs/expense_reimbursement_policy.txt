NORTHWIND TECHNOLOGIES — EXPENSE REIMBURSEMENT POLICY
Document Owner: Finance
Effective Date: January 1, 2026
Applies To: All employees incurring business-related expenses

1. PURPOSE
This policy establishes guidelines for submitting and approving reimbursement of legitimate business expenses incurred by employees.

2. GENERAL PRINCIPLES
All expenses must be reasonable, necessary for business purposes, and supported by an itemized receipt. Expenses without a valid receipt will not be reimbursed except for per-diem meal allowances under $25, which may be self-certified.

3. TRAVEL EXPENSES
3.1 Airfare
Employees must book economy class for flights under 6 hours and premium economy for flights exceeding 6 hours, using the company's approved travel booking platform. Business class requires VP-level pre-approval and is reserved for flights exceeding 9 hours.

3.2 Accommodation
Hotel bookings should not exceed $250/night in major metro areas (e.g., New York, San Francisco, London) or $150/night in other locations, unless a client event requires a specific venue.

3.3 Ground Transportation
Ride-share, taxi, and rental car expenses are reimbursable for business travel. Employees are encouraged to use public transit where practical. Personal vehicle use is reimbursed at $0.67 per mile.

3.4 Meals
Daily meal allowance while traveling is capped at $75/day (breakfast $15, lunch $20, dinner $40), inclusive of tax and tip. Alcohol is not reimbursable except at approved client entertainment events, up to $50/person.

4. CLIENT ENTERTAINMENT
Client entertainment expenses (meals, event tickets, etc.) require pre-approval from a manager for amounts exceeding $200 per event and must include the names of attendees and business purpose in the expense report.

5. SUBMISSION AND APPROVAL
Expense reports must be submitted within 30 days of the expense being incurred, via the company's expense management system. Reports submitted after 60 days require Finance Director approval and may be denied at Finance's discretion. Manager approval is required for all reports; Finance approval is additionally required for reports exceeding $1,000.

6. NON-REIMBURSABLE EXPENSES
The following are not reimbursable under any circumstances: personal entertainment, traffic or parking fines, spa or gym services, personal grooming, lost personal item replacement, and travel insurance for personal trips extended beyond business travel dates.

7. CORPORATE CARDS
Employees issued a corporate card must use it for all eligible business expenses where possible and reconcile transactions monthly. Personal use of a corporate card, even if reimbursed later, is a policy violation and may result in card revocation.

8. AUDITS
Finance may audit expense reports at random or based on risk indicators (e.g., expenses just under approval thresholds, frequent late submissions). Employees found to have submitted fraudulent expense claims are subject to disciplinary action up to termination and possible legal action.
