NORTHWIND TECHNOLOGIES — REMOTE WORK POLICY
Document Owner: Human Resources
Effective Date: February 1, 2026
Applies To: All eligible full-time employees

1. PURPOSE
This policy defines eligibility, expectations, and guidelines for employees working remotely, whether on a hybrid or fully remote basis.

2. ELIGIBILITY
Remote work eligibility is determined by role type. Roles are classified into three categories:
- Fully Remote: employee works from a non-office location full-time (e.g., Engineering, Customer Support for approved regions)
- Hybrid: employee is expected in the office a minimum of 2 days per week (e.g., Sales, Product Management, Marketing)
- Onsite Required: employee must work from the office full-time due to role requirements (e.g., Facilities, Hardware Lab roles)

New employees must complete a 90-day onboarding period working primarily onsite or hybrid before transitioning to a fully remote arrangement, unless the role was hired as fully remote from the start.

3. HYBRID SCHEDULE EXPECTATIONS
Hybrid employees must coordinate their in-office days with their team to ensure at least 2 overlapping in-office days per week for team collaboration. Core collaboration hours (10:00 AM–4:00 PM local time) apply to all hybrid and remote employees regardless of location.

4. EQUIPMENT AND EXPENSES
Northwind Technologies provides a one-time home office setup stipend of $500 for employees approved for fully remote work, covering items such as a monitor, chair, or desk. Ongoing internet costs are reimbursed up to $50/month with a submitted receipt. Company laptops and required software licenses are provided regardless of work location.

5. WORKING FROM ABROAD
Employees wishing to work remotely from a country other than their contracted country of employment must obtain prior written approval from HR and Legal, due to tax, visa, and data protection implications. Unapproved international remote work is a policy violation and may affect payroll, benefits, and data compliance status.

6. PERFORMANCE AND AVAILABILITY
Remote and hybrid employees are expected to maintain the same availability, responsiveness, and performance standards as onsite employees. Managers may request a return to onsite work if performance concerns arise that are, in the manager's reasonable judgment, linked to the remote work arrangement.

7. SECURITY REQUIREMENTS
Remote employees must use company-issued VPN software when accessing internal systems, keep work devices password-protected, and avoid conducting company business over unsecured public Wi-Fi networks. Any lost or stolen company device must be reported to IT Security within 24 hours.

8. POLICY REVIEW
This policy is reviewed annually and may be adjusted based on business needs, team feedback, and evolving best practices in distributed work.
