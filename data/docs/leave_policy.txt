NORTHWIND TECHNOLOGIES — EMPLOYEE LEAVE POLICY
Document Owner: Human Resources
Effective Date: January 1, 2026
Applies To: All full-time and part-time employees

1. PURPOSE
This policy outlines the types of leave available to Northwind Technologies employees, eligibility requirements, and the process for requesting time off.

2. TYPES OF LEAVE

2.1 Annual Leave (Paid Time Off)
Full-time employees accrue 20 days of paid annual leave per calendar year, credited at a rate of 1.67 days per completed month of service. Part-time employees accrue leave on a pro-rata basis according to their contracted hours. Unused annual leave may be carried forward into the next calendar year up to a maximum of 5 days; any balance beyond this cap is forfeited on December 31st unless otherwise approved by a department head.

2.2 Sick Leave
Employees are entitled to 12 days of paid sick leave per year. A medical certificate is required for any sick leave exceeding 2 consecutive working days. Sick leave does not carry forward to the following year and is not paid out upon resignation or termination.

2.3 Maternity Leave
Eligible employees are entitled to 26 weeks of paid maternity leave, in line with statutory requirements. Employees must notify their manager and HR at least 8 weeks before the expected leave start date. Maternity leave may begin up to 8 weeks before the expected delivery date.

2.4 Paternity Leave
Eligible employees are entitled to 2 weeks of paid paternity leave, to be taken within 6 months of the child's birth or adoption.

2.5 Bereavement Leave
Employees may take up to 5 paid days of bereavement leave in the event of the death of an immediate family member (spouse, child, parent, or sibling), and up to 2 paid days for extended family members.

2.6 Unpaid Leave
Employees who have exhausted all paid leave categories may request unpaid leave of absence. Requests must be submitted to HR at least 4 weeks in advance and are subject to manager and HR approval. Unpaid leave exceeding 30 consecutive days may affect benefits continuity; employees should consult HR before proceeding.

3. REQUESTING LEAVE
All leave requests must be submitted through the internal HR portal at least 2 weeks in advance for planned leave (annual leave). Sick leave should be reported to the direct manager as early as possible, ideally before the start of the working day. Leave requests are approved or denied based on team staffing needs and business continuity requirements.

4. PUBLIC HOLIDAYS
Northwind Technologies observes 12 public holidays per year, as published annually by HR. Employees required to work on a public holiday are entitled to a compensatory day off or holiday pay at 1.5x the standard daily rate, per manager discretion and local labor law.

5. LEAVE DURING PROBATION
Employees within their first 90 days of employment (probation period) are eligible to accrue annual leave but may only use up to 5 days during this period, except in cases of documented medical emergency.

6. POLICY REVIEW
This policy is reviewed annually by the HR department and may be updated to reflect changes in labor law or company practice. Employees will be notified of material changes via company-wide email at least 30 days before the change takes effect.
