NORTHWIND TECHNOLOGIES — CODE OF CONDUCT
Document Owner: Human Resources & Legal
Effective Date: January 1, 2026
Applies To: All employees, contractors, and interns

1. PURPOSE
This Code of Conduct sets out the standards of behavior expected of everyone working at or on behalf of Northwind Technologies, to maintain a respectful, safe, and productive workplace.

2. CORE PRINCIPLES
2.1 Respect and Inclusion
Employees must treat colleagues, clients, and partners with respect regardless of race, gender, religion, sexual orientation, disability, age, or nationality. Discrimination, harassment, or bullying of any kind will not be tolerated and may result in disciplinary action up to and including termination.

2.2 Integrity
Employees are expected to act honestly in all business dealings, avoid conflicts of interest, and disclose any potential conflicts to their manager or HR promptly. Falsifying company records, expense reports, or performance data is grounds for immediate termination.

2.3 Confidentiality
Employees must protect confidential company information, customer data, and trade secrets, both during and after employment. Unauthorized disclosure of confidential information is a serious violation and may result in legal action in addition to termination.

3. WORKPLACE BEHAVIOR
3.1 Anti-Harassment
Northwind Technologies has zero tolerance for sexual harassment, verbal abuse, or any conduct that creates a hostile work environment. Employees who experience or witness harassment should report it immediately to HR or through the anonymous ethics hotline.

3.2 Substance Use
Employees must not be under the influence of alcohol or illegal drugs during working hours or while representing the company at client events. Moderate alcohol consumption at approved company-sponsored social events is permitted.

3.3 Use of Company Assets
Company equipment, software licenses, and systems must be used primarily for business purposes. Incidental personal use is permitted provided it does not interfere with work responsibilities or violate security policy.

4. CONFLICTS OF INTEREST
Employees must not engage in outside employment, consulting, or business activities that compete with Northwind Technologies or interfere with their job duties without prior written approval from their manager and HR.

5. REPORTING VIOLATIONS
Employees who witness a violation of this Code of Conduct should report it to their manager, HR, or via the anonymous ethics hotline available on the company intranet. Retaliation against anyone who reports a violation in good faith is strictly prohibited and is itself a violation of this Code.

6. DISCIPLINARY ACTION
Violations of this Code of Conduct are handled on a case-by-case basis and may result in a verbal warning, written warning, suspension, or termination of employment, depending on severity. Serious violations (fraud, harassment, safety violations) may result in immediate termination without prior warning.

7. ACKNOWLEDGEMENT
All employees are required to read and acknowledge this Code of Conduct annually via the HR portal as a condition of continued employment.
